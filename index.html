profile

Three years spent making data arrive on time and mean the right thing.

I work across SQL, ETL/ELT pipelines, cloud data platforms and the applications that sit on top of them. Day to day that means Snowflake and dbt for staged-to-mart transformations, incremental processing and analytical modeling, and PySpark on Databricks for IoT telemetry.

My foundation is SQL — Snowflake, SQL Server and PostgreSQL — and a lot of my value shows up in the unglamorous parts: tracing why a scheduled load failed, reading a query profile to work out why a warehouse is burning credits, and designing a model whose grain still makes sense a year later.

Before data engineering I built backends. That still helps: I know what the service consuming a mart needs, and I've been on the other side of a slow query.
